# GRC Portfolio - Financial Services & Regulation

A comprehensive portfolio showcasing applied research projects in Information Systems, Governance, Risk Management, Compliance (GRC), and Financial Services Regulation.

## Overview

This portfolio demonstrates practical implementation of regulatory frameworks and information security best practices within financial services organisations. Each project addresses real-world challenges across Irish, UK, and EU jurisdictions.

## Projects

### 1. Enterprise Risk Register
**Module:** IT Governance & Risk Management  
**Framework:** ISO 31000 & COSO ERM  
**Status:** Active

Centralised platform for identifying, assessing and monitoring organisational risks with automated heat maps and mitigation tracking.

**Key Features:**
- Risk identification and categorisation
- Qualitative and quantitative assessment
- Risk appetite threshold monitoring
- Mitigation plan tracking
- Board-level reporting

---

### 2. GRC Compliance Hub
**Module:** Governance & Regulation in Financial Services  
**Framework:** Integrated GRC  
**Status:** Active

Integrated platform unifying governance frameworks, risk registers, compliance obligations and internal audit management.

**Key Features:**
- Policy management with approval workflows
- Control testing and monitoring
- Audit planning and execution
- Regulatory change management
- Compliance reporting

---

### 3. DORA Compliance Monitor
**Module:** Financial Services Legislation  
**Framework:** Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA)  
**Status:** Active

Regulatory compliance tool for financial entities monitoring ICT risk, incident reporting and third-party dependencies under DORA.

**Key Features:**
- ICT risk management requirements
- Major incident reporting (thresholds)
- Digital operational resilience testing (TLPT)
- ICT third-party risk management
- RTS mapping and notification workflows

---

### 4. ISO 27001 ISMS Manager
**Module:** IT Security, Compliance & Privacy  
**Framework:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022  
**Status:** Active

System for implementing and maintaining ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS with SOA management, risk treatment and audit readiness.

**Key Features:**
- Statement of Applicability (93 controls)
- Information security risk assessments
- Risk treatment plans
- Security policy management
- Internal audit scheduling
- Gap analysis tools

- Interpret regulatory requirements for compliance
